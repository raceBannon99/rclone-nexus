Privacy policy

rclone-nexus is used only by its owner to back up the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It requests only the drive.file scope, which limits access to files the app itself creates.
  • No data is collected from, or about, anyone else.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Backups are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload. Credentials are stored only on that computer.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/raceBannon99/rclone-nexus.
